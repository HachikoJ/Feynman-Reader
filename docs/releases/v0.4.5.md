# v0.4.5

本版本统一优化全站即时提示，鼠标移到需要说明的交互控件上时立即显示文字，不再等待浏览器原生 `title`。

## 即时提示

- 鼠标进入按钮后立即显示提示，在同一组操作之间移动时会即时切换内容。
- 书架卡片右上角的阅读、编辑、删除，以及书架顶部的新增、导入、书单、批量管理和视图切换均已接入统一提示。
- 阅读工具栏、费曼小助手、设置、账号中心、导入窗口和其他紧凑或纯图标控件也使用相同行为。
- 提示会自动避让视口边缘，适配浅色与深色主题，并支持键盘焦点显示与 `Escape` 关闭。
- 禁用控件仍可显示说明；触摸操作不触发悬停提示。

本版本不涉及数据库结构、账号权限或已保存凭据行为变更。

## English

Version 0.4.5 replaces delayed native browser tooltips with immediate hints on interactive controls throughout the app.

Moving the pointer onto a control reveals its hint at once, and moving between related actions updates the hint immediately. This covers the bookshelf card actions, top toolbar, reader controls, Feynman Assistant, settings, Account Center, import dialogs, and other compact or icon-only controls. The tooltip stays within the viewport, adapts to light and dark themes, appears for keyboard focus, closes with `Escape`, works for disabled controls, and does not interfere with touch input.

No database schema, account permission, or stored credential behavior changes.
