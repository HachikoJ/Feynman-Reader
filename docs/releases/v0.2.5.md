# v0.2.5

在页脚的特别支持区与版权备案信息之间加入观猹产品徽章，点击在新标签页打开费曼读书助手的观猹产品页。使用用户提供的官方图片和链接，保留来源参数。

亮色主题显示白版图片，暗色和 cyber 主题显示黑版图片。徽章居中，最大宽度 360px，在手机上等比例缩小，并提供键盘焦点提示。

浏览器检查覆盖 375、768、1024、1440px 和三个主题的 12 种组合，图片正常加载，无水平溢出，链接地址和键盘访问通过。无数据库、登录或权限变更；保留 v0.2.4 及部署前私有备份，参见[版本发布与恢复](../operations/releases-and-rollback.md)。

## English

Add the supplied Watcha product badge between the footer support section and copyright information. The link opens Feynman Reader's Watcha product page in a new tab and preserves the supplied attribution parameters.

Use the white artwork in light mode and black artwork in dark and cyber modes. The centered badge retains its original proportions at a maximum width of 360px and scales down on mobile. Keyboard focus is visible.

Browser checks cover four viewport widths and three themes, including image loading, link destination, keyboard access and horizontal overflow. No database, authentication or permission changes. Retain v0.2.4 and private deployment backups for rollback.
