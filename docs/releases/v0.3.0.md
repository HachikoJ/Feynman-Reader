# v0.3.0

本版本把平台从「读完再问答」推进到「先读原文，再用费曼学习法复盘」：导入的电子书可以在站内按章节阅读、划线、写笔记、加书签，也可以把其他阅读器里的划线与笔记导入进来，一起用于复述、提问与 AI 核对。

## 在站内读原文

- 支持 EPUB、MOBI / AZW3 / AZW / PRC、FB2、DOCX / DOC、HTML / XHTML、RTF、PDF、TXT、Markdown 和 JSON。EPUB、MOBI 会自动带出书名、作者、封面与章节；DOCX / DOC 与 PDF 会转成可阅读、可划线的正文。
- 阅读器提供目录、字号与阅读主题、书签、关键词检索、键盘左右方向键翻节，并在侧栏给出划线、书签与检索面板。
- 书架同时显示原文阅读进度与费曼阶段进度，两者是独立指标，不会被 AI 评分替代。
- 「添加书籍」与「上传文档」合并为同一个导入窗口：导入文件之后，书名、作者、封面和标签仍然可以自由修改。

## 划线、笔记与书签

- 选中原文即可划四种颜色的线、写笔记、加标签；单个标签 24 字以内，每条划线最多 8 个，输入时会提示全书已有标签。
- 点击已有划线可以改色、补备注、增删标签或删除；划线面板支持按章节和标签筛选，书签可以补充备注与颜色，单本最多保留 500 个书签。
- 划线保留章节出处与字符位置，在「我的笔记」中与书签一起聚合，并可按来源（原文划线 / 外部导入 / 手工笔记）和标签筛选。

## 从其他阅读器导入划线与笔记

- Readwise、Zotero：通过官方 API 直接读取，访问令牌只保存在当前浏览器，不上传到本平台服务器。
- 微信读书、Kindle、Apple Books、得到、Google Play Books、KOReader：使用各平台官方导出，粘贴或上传文本即可解析；「平台支持说明」列出每个平台的导出步骤和官方链接。
- Get笔记、有道云笔记、掌阅、京东读书、番茄、QQ 阅读等：没有面向个人开发者开放的接口，也没有通用导出格式，本版本暂不接入，窗口内说明了原因。
- 项目不使用 cookie 抓取、模拟登录或逆向方案，也不接入以此实现的第三方工具；各平台以官方导出方式为准。

## 原文引用与 AI 核对

- 费曼复述与 AI 点评可以引用你自己的划线（`[H#]`），在「核对原文证据」里回到对应段落与章节。
- 阶段分析提示词要求区分「原文能读到的观点」与「书外观点」，没有原文时不得生成原文编号；来源不确凿时写成有多种解读，不编造引文、页码或参考文献。

## 兼容与数据

- 备份文件接受新增的章节、书签、阅读进度、笔记来源与标签字段，仍可导入 v0.2.x 的备份；导出会带上这些阅读数据。
- 本版本不涉及数据库结构、账号权限或已保存凭据行为变更。升级前保留 v0.2.7 的部署快照与私有备份，参见[版本发布与恢复](../operations/releases-and-rollback.md)。

## English

This release moves the product from "read first, ask afterwards" to a full reading loop: imported books can be read chapter by chapter inside the app, highlighted, annotated, and bookmarked, and highlights from other readers can be imported to support Feynman practice, questions, and AI review.

## Read the source text in the app

- Import EPUB, MOBI / AZW3 / AZW / PRC, FB2, DOCX / DOC, HTML / XHTML, RTF, PDF, TXT, Markdown, and JSON. EPUB and MOBI bring title, author, cover, and chapters automatically; DOCX / DOC and PDF become readable, highlightable text.
- The reader provides a table of contents, font-size and reader-theme controls, bookmarks, keyword search, keyboard section navigation, and side panels for highlights, bookmarks, and search results.
- The bookshelf shows source reading progress next to the six-phase Feynman progress. The two are separate indicators and neither replaces AI scores.
- Adding a book and uploading a document now share one import dialog: after importing a file, title, author, cover, and tags remain freely editable.

## Highlights, notes, and bookmarks

- Select source text to draw four highlight colors, write a note, and attach tags. A tag is limited to 24 characters with at most 8 tags per highlight, and existing tags in the book are suggested while typing.
- Selecting an existing highlight allows changing its color, adding a note, editing its tags, or deleting it. The highlight panel filters by chapter and tag; bookmarks accept a note and color and are capped at 500 per book.
- Highlights keep their chapter and character offsets, appear together with bookmarks in My Notes, and can be filtered by source (source highlight, imported, manual note) and by tag.

## Import highlights and notes from other readers

- Readwise and Zotero are read through their official APIs. Access tokens stay in the browser and are never uploaded to this service.
- WeChat Reading, Kindle, Apple Books, Dedao, Google Play Books, and KOReader are supported through their official exports: paste or upload the exported text and the importer parses it. The platform support tab lists the export steps and official links for each one.
- Get Notes, Youdao Note, iReader, JD Read, Fanqie, QQ Reader, and similar apps publish no API for individual developers and no common export format, so this release does not integrate them; the dialog explains why.
- The project uses no cookie scraping, scripted sign-in, or reverse engineering, and does not integrate third-party tools built on them.

## Source references and AI review

- Feynman practice and AI feedback can cite your own highlights through `[H#]` references, returning to the matching passage and chapter under "Verify source evidence".
- Phase prompts must separate claims readable in the source from outside-source judgments, must not invent source ids when no source is provided, and must avoid fabricated quotations, page numbers, or references when the provenance is uncertain.

## Compatibility and data

- Backups accept the new chapter, bookmark, reading-progress, note-source, and tag fields and still import v0.2.x backups; exports include the new reading data.
- This release changes no database schema, account permissions, or stored credential behavior. Retain the v0.2.7 deployment snapshot and private backups before upgrading; see the [version and recovery guide](../operations/releases-and-rollback.md).
