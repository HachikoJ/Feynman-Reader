# Watcha Icons

Watcha artwork supplied by the project owner from the Watcha icon package on 2026-09-09. The two SVGs are unchanged copies of the supplied round and rounded-square variants.

- `icon-round.svg`: sign-in controls and default avatars for Watcha accounts.
- `icon-rounded.svg`: Watcha sign-in page and onboarding identity.

Preserve the original colors, proportions and built-in background in both light and dark themes. Do not invert or recolor the artwork. User-supplied avatars take priority; these assets are display fallbacks and must never be saved to user profiles. Password accounts and local previews keep their generic identity. TokenDance and Feynman Assistant retain their own marks.
